iPhone

Get Ravenry's alerts on your iPhone, set up from Ravenry's settings.

How to set up alerts
  1. In Ravenry on your Mac, open Alerts and choose Add an iPhone.
  2. Scan the code with your iPhone's camera.
  3. Get ntfy from the App Store, paste the code in it and send a test.

This is an early test

Expect rough edges and the odd crash. The Mac app updates itself; for Android, download the new APK and it installs over the old one. Tell us what breaks, or what confuses you: report a problem.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later
  • Claude Code or Codex, installed and signed in
  • For the phone: the same Wi-Fi as your Mac, or Tailscale
