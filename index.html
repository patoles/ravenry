Ask

They ask.
You answer.

When an agent needs a choice, a go-ahead or a secret, it stops and asks. The question shows up on your Mac and your phone at the same time.

Remember

It remembers
what you decided.

Every answer you give goes into Decisions, and agents check them before they ask again. Each project keeps a short brief. When a session runs low on room, it hands off to a fresh one that picks up where it stopped.

Board

Every session.
One list.

The sessions waiting on you stay on top, whether they run on this Mac or on another one you reach over ssh.

Needs youA question, a command to approve, a finished task. Answer it and it moves on.
WorkingBusy agents, with how much room each one has left.
IdleFolded away until you want them.
Clean up

Clean up idle sessions.
In one click.

Old sessions pile up and hold on to memory. Ravenry ticks the ones that are safe to close, shows what each one holds and closes them together. Changed your mind? Reopen it.

Phone

Leave your desk.
Not your agents.

The Android app shows every project and its live screen. When an agent asks, answer right from the notification. On iPhone, you get the alerts too.

Terminal

A real terminal,
made for the Mac.

Native and drawn on the GPU, so typing feels instant. Tabs and splits for everything else you run. Quit the app or update it: your sessions keep running.
