# Code of conduct

Ravenry wants everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests and
anywhere else the project meets, to feel welcome.

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content, threats or personal
  attacks, and no publishing anyone's private information.
- Respect a "no" and stop when asked.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull
requests, and block anyone who does not follow this. To report a problem
privately, use the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub's private
reporting) and say it is about conduct.
