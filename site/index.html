One place for every decision.

On this board, Claude Code is waiting for you to choose how a boat should tilt. Codex is still working on sync settings. Ravenry uses agent events and the live terminal screen to tell which sessions need you, including those on machines connected over SSH.

Needs youSee the question, the command to approve or the work ready to review.

IdleReopen the list when you want to pick up a session.

They ask.
You decide.

Each request becomes a card made for that kind of decision: options as buttons, a command with its folder and warnings, designs side by side. Open the live terminal when you need to look closer. Your response goes back to the session that asked.

Decide once.

If you decide the boat should stay level on gentle slopes, save that choice in the project’s Decisions. Future sessions can check it before asking again. A hand-off brief tells the next session what’s done and what to do next.

Decide from anywhere.

Pair your iPhone or Android phone with your Mac over Wi-Fi or Tailscale. Answer a question from its notification, or open the app to see the live terminal and start a new session.

Close idle sessions together.

Old sessions pile up and hold on to memory. Ravenry ticks the ones that are safe to close, shows what each one holds and closes them together. You can reopen any of them later.
