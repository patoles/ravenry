# Trademark Policy

## Trademarks

The name **"Ravenry"** and its logo (the raven icon in `brand/ravenry/`) are
trademarks of Simon Patole.

## What this means

The source code of Ravenry is available under the Apache License 2.0. That
licence grants broad permission to use, modify and distribute the code, but
it **does not** grant permission to use the Ravenry trademarks (Section 6 of
the licence).

## Guidelines

### You may

- Use the name "Ravenry" to say truthfully where your software comes from
  (for example "based on Ravenry" or "a fork of Ravenry").
- Use the name in discussions, blog posts and tutorials about this project.
- Use the code in your own projects under the terms of the Apache 2.0
  licence.

### You may not

- Name your fork or derivative work "Ravenry" or anything confusingly
  similar, or publish it on npm under that name.
- Use the Ravenry logo or icons in your fork or derivative work.
- Imply that your fork or derivative work is the official Ravenry project,
  or is endorsed by its maintainers.
- Use the Ravenry trademarks in any way that suggests an official
  affiliation or endorsement.

### Forks and derivative works

If you fork Ravenry or build a derivative work from it, you must:

1. Choose a different name for your project.
2. Remove the Ravenry logo and branding (replace the icons in
   `brand/ravenry/` and the name in the page and the terminal output).
3. Say clearly that your project is a fork or derivative of Ravenry.

This is the approach projects like Firefox, Rust and Kubernetes take: the
code is open, the brand is protected.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use of the Ravenry trademarks is allowed, open
an issue in this repository.
